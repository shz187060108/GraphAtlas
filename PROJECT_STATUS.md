## Verified three-seed evidence

The final one-layer Atlas-Het mechanism matrix contains nine matched runs. Full GraphAtlas has mean test metric 0.739, mean boundary accuracy 0.737 and nonlinear intervention error 6.7e-8. No transport has 0.703, 0.694 and 6.6e-2. Free transitions have 0.725, 0.675 and 1.43e-1. All five automated scientific gates pass. Full GraphAtlas is approximately 29% slower than no transport in this small CPU setting.

The result is notable because the exact coordinate-invariance mechanism no longer trades away mean clean-coordinate performance in the matched one-layer study. The sample is still only three seeds on one synthetic setting. Confidence intervals remain wide, and the path-consistency and metric-compatibility diagnostics are not better than the ablations under the selected low regularization.
